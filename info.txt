USAGE :
    ./my_hunter
Launch a game where you're a pasta hunter, isn't it weird ?
That's the way you are anyway, don't deny it.

Pasta will appear on the screen, destroy them using the mouse click !
To quit earlier a running party, use (CTRL + Q).
To change current music station, use (CTRL + M).

Be aware hunter, some pasta could make use of an unpredicatble behavior.
Ducks can do loopings, balls bounce and get smaller with time, 
 and forks are thrown fast but then slow down.

Don't let pasta reach the castle tower.
    Five of them get in and you're out !

If you need a break, don't worry,
 just go to an other window and the game will pause itself.
